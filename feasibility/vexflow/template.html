VexFlow feasibility test: makam notation

Pieces from SymbTr, drawn with VexFlow 5.9 and its Bravura font. Click a note to select it; arrow keys move it, keys 1–9 set a flat of that many commas, Shift+1–9 a sharp, 0 a natural; I inserts a note, Delete removes one. Play (or Space) starts from the selected note; the sound is synthesized at exact comma pitches.
