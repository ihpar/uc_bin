# Baseline results, first round (archived)

Runs are in `runs/archive_v1/`. Known issues of this round, fixed afterwards:

- Training windows of long pieces were shifted to fill the context, so the end of a piece always sat at the last position and piece openings were rarely seen. This affects how models start and end pieces (scenario 2), and slightly the likelihood numbers.
- Usul-constrained decoding did not allow a piece to end on a completed measure.

Scenario 1 results and the comparisons between encodings and model sizes are not affected in direction.

Generated by `scripts/05_summarize_runs.py`. Mean ± standard deviation over seeds.
